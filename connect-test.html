Gringotts Connect test (throwaway)

Tests only the Connect step, against a test vault with no data. Your own macros and variables aren't touched: this uses gr_url and gr_secret only.

1. Delete any old Gringotts connect macro in MacroDroid first. Then add the Gringotts connect macro: tap, open it with MacroDroid, Save.

Gringotts connect macro

2. Tap Connect. In the share list, pick MacroDroid (it may be called “Text shared to MacroDroid”).

3. Within a few seconds a notification should say Gringotts connected, and the Gringotts connect macro should switch itself off.
