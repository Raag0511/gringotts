🔒 Your privacy

Only you can see your data. Not the person who shared Gringotts, not anyone else.

Nothing leaves your Google account, except feedback you choose to send.

You can remove Gringotts' access any time.

Read more about your privacy
  • The warning: Google shows it for every app that isn't on its store. "Unsafe" is Google's standard word for any unverified app, not a finding about this one.
  • Your data: everything is kept in your own Google Sheet and Drive. Nothing is sent to anyone else. To see or remove the app's access: Google Account › Security › "Your connections to third-party apps".
  • Updates: they come from the person who shared Gringotts with you, but they can't see your data. Once a day your app moves to their latest release by itself. They can't open your sheet, and nothing you enter passes through their account. Like any app that updates itself, it works on trust, so set it up with someone you know.
