Gringotts privacy

Last updated 22 November 2026

In short

  • Your data stays in your own Google account. Nobody else can see it, including the person who shared Gringotts with you.
  • There are no Gringotts servers, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing is sold.
  • You can remove Gringotts' access, or delete everything, at any time.

Who makes Gringotts

Gringotts is a personal expense tracker made by one person and shared with family and friends. It is not a company or a commercial service.

Where your data lives

When you set Gringotts up, it makes a Google Sheet and a small app (an Apps Script project) in your own Google account. The app runs as you, inside Google. Your expenses, cards, notes and settings are kept only in that sheet and its settings, in your own Drive.

Your bank SMS go from your phone (through MacroDroid on Android) straight to your own app's link. They are not sent anywhere else.

Gringotts' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What leaves your account

  • Feedback you choose to send (Settings › Send feedback) goes to a Google Form read by the person who shared Gringotts. Only what you type is sent.
  • Nothing else. No usage data, no crash reports, no copies of your entries.

Updates

Once a day, the Gringotts setup app (running as you) checks the public release note and, if there is a new version, moves your app to it. An update only brings new app code. It never sends your data anywhere, and the person who publishes updates cannot see your sheet. A release that needs a new permission waits until you allow it.

Removing access, and deleting everything

  • Remove access: Google Account › Security › "Your connections to third-party apps & services" › remove each Gringotts entry (there can be two: the setup page and your own app). Gringotts stops at once.
  • Delete everything: delete the "Gringotts" sheet from your Drive (the app inside it goes with it), then empty your Drive bin. Nothing is kept anywhere else.
