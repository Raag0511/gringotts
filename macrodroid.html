Get MacroDroid first

Connect needs the free MacroDroid app on this phone. It passes your bank SMS to your own Gringotts, and nothing else.

Open the Play Store

Then:

1. Go back to Gringotts › Settings › Connect this phone.

2. Add the three Gringotts macros (tap each, open with MacroDroid, Save).

3. Tap Connect again and pick MacroDroid in the share list.

This page was opened because MacroDroid isn't installed. Nothing private is on it or sent from it.
